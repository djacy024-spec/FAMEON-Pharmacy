Common questions

How fast is delivery?

Average delivery time is 28 minutes for in-stock items. Orders placed after 10 PM are delivered next morning. Free delivery on orders over $25.

Do I need a prescription for all medicines?

OTC products like vitamins, pain relievers, and skincare don't require a prescription. Prescription medications need a valid Rx from a licensed doctor, which you can upload on our platform.

Are all products genuine?

100%. Every product is sourced directly from manufacturers or authorized distributors. We're FDA licensed and GMP certified with full batch tracking.

Can I return products?

Unopened items can be returned within 7 days. For damaged or incorrect items, we offer immediate replacement or full refund. Prescription medicines cannot be returned due to safety regulations.

How does online consultation work?

Book a session with a licensed pharmacist via chat, call, or video — available 24/7. They can help with medication queries, interactions, dosage guidance, and OTC recommendations.
